Channel- locked vs. direct/hybrid P. 0 24:T4b7,For decades, the way enterprise manufa c turers sold complex products was simple. A buyer found a brand. The brand handed them a catalog. The catalog reached a dealer. The dealer closed the sale. That model still works in some places. It works less well almost everywhere else. The buyer who arrives on a manufacturer's website today has already done much of what a dealer used to do. They've researched alternatives, narrowed options, and looked for prices. What they haven't done is fill out a form and wait three days for a callback. Across this audit of 100 enterprise manufacturers, the gap between what buyers expect online and what manufacturers actually deliver is the most c onsistent finding. The median Buyer Enablement Score across the cohort is 2.25 out of 10. The harder question is how manufacturers do more on the website without disintermediating the channels that still close most of the revenue. A few sites in this audit point to a working pattern. The website does the early work. The channel still closes the deal. We hope this report is useful to the manufacturers in our cohort and to the broader category.
