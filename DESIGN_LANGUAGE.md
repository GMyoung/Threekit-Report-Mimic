# Threekit Report Design Language

## Core Impression

The report uses a restrained B2B research style: large editorial typography, generous whitespace, muted paper backgrounds, and a tight accent palette. The pages feel more like a branded strategy deck than a traditional PDF report.

## Palette

- Paper: warm off-white, roughly `#f3f5ee` to `#f7f7f0`
- Ink: deep green-teal, roughly `#173235`
- Accent: salmon/coral, roughly `#ea8768`
- Secondary fields: pale blue-gray, roughly `#e8f1f0`
- Muted text and rules: gray-green, roughly `#aebbb6`

## Typography

- Oversized condensed sans headings dominate each chapter opener.
- Italic words are used as strategic emphasis, often in coral.
- Eyebrow labels are small uppercase, widely tracked, and coral.
- Body text is compact and workmanlike, with short paragraphs and narrow line lengths.
- Score numbers are oversized and separated from explanatory text.

## Layout System

- Page format is portrait and editorial, with a strong fixed grid.
- Many pages use a split between a dominant left title/stat area and compact right-side explanation blocks.
- Chapter pages use large coral numerals, all-caps chapter labels, and horizontal rules.
- Tables and scorecards use thin rules, low-contrast panels, and restrained fills.
- Footer metadata is quiet and often gray-green.

## Graphic Motifs

- Large off-page coral circles on covers and section pages.
- Small coral dots and pill-like bars as emphasis marks.
- Rounded scorecards and data panels, usually with 4px to 8px radius.
- Dark teal full-page sections for major synthesis moments.
- Sparse iconography: the brand mark appears cleanly without extra ornament.

## Website Approach

The website should not primarily reproduce PDF pages as page images. It should use the PDF as source material and translate the same language into a longform interactive report: warm paper, deep teal chrome, coral emphasis, compact controls, sticky navigation, progress, searchable sections, and large readable chapter openings.

Every source section must remain present and copyable. The readable layer may reorganize spacing and paragraph flow for comprehension, but the canonical extracted text remains available for exact verification.
